Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Local Document Processing

freepdfs.tools operates entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript WebAssembly and memory streams. Documents uploaded to any tool (Merge, Split, Compress, Organize) are processed locally on your device. Files are never transmitted, stored, or processed on external servers.

2. Advertising and Cookies

We use third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable information (such as IP address, browser type, and click activity) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on user visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

3. Analytics and Operational Logs

We may collect basic, non-identifying telemetry (such as page visit counts, device orientation, and generalized error rates) to maintain and optimize site performance. No document metadata or personal information is attached to these logs.

4. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected directly on this page.
